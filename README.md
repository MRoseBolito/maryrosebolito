## 1. Your photo (already added)
Your photo is in place at `assets/rose-photo.png` and shows automatically in
the purple hero frame. Since the photo has a transparent background, it sits
on top of the purple gradient card like a color-blocked headshot. Want a
different crop or photo later? Just replace the file (keep the same filename)
or swap the `src` in `index.html`.

## 2. Deploy to GitHub Pages (same as your current site)
1. Go to your `mrosebolito.github.io` repository (or the `maryrosebolito`
   folder/branch it currently uses).
2. Replace the existing files with these: `index.html`, `style.css`,
   `script.js`, and the `assets` folder.
3. Commit and push. GitHub Pages will rebuild automatically — give it a
   minute or two, then refresh your live URL.
